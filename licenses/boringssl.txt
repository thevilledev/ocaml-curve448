BoringSSL
=========

Parts of the C backend follow BoringSSL's crypto/curve25519 code, in the
revision vendored by mirage-crypto-ec (BoringSSL commits c47bfce06 and
0fc57bef18), which was distributed under the ISC-style license below:

- lib/c/native/curve448.h: the X448 Montgomery ladder is adapted from
  x25519_scalar_mult_generic (statement order and temporaries, with the
  Curve448 constant and scalar size);
- lib/c/native/field448.h: the tight/loose field element types and the
  fe_* wrapper naming around fiat-crypto;
- lib/c/native/edwards448.h: the ge_p2/ge_p3/ge_p1p1/ge_precomp/ge_cached
  point representations and conversion structure, which BoringSSL in turn
  took from the public-domain ref10 Ed25519 code in SUPERCOP.

The edwards448 formulas, scalar arithmetic, tables and Ed448 logic are not
derived from BoringSSL.
