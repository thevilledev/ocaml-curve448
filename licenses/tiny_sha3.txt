tiny_sha3
=========

keccak_f1600 in lib/c/native/shake256.h follows sha3_keccakf from tiny_sha3
by Markku-Juhani O. Saarinen (https://github.com/mjosaarinen/tiny_sha3): the
same loop structure over the FIPS 202 round constants, rotation offsets and
pi lane order. The sponge, padding and squeeze code around it is not derived
